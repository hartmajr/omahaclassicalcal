JULY 26

Nebraska Crossroads Music Festival
Women’s Bandura Ensemble of North America

Sunday, July 26th — 11:30am — SOLD OUT

Tickets for this event are sold out. You can check out the rest of the Crossroads Music Festival lineup at NebraskaMusicFest.org. There are 19 free events across Omaha and Lincoln from July 19 - August 5.

We’re excited to partner with the Nebraska Crossroads Music Festival to bring you a performance by the Women’s Bandura Ensemble. This group celebrates a unique Ukrainian instrument whose sound carries centuries of history, identity, and cultural memory. The ensemble is composed of women from nine cities in North America, including Toronto, New York, and Chicago.

Tickets are SOLD OUT.

AUG 6

Smolder Book Launch
Winner of the Ex Ophidia Press Poetry Contest

Thursday, August 6th— 6:00-8:00pm

Join us for a poetry reading celebrating the launch of Smolder by Colleen Morton Busch. Busch is the 2025 winner of the Richard-Gabriel Rummonds Poetry Prize, an annual poetry book contest by Ex Ophidia Press, an Omaha-based independent poetry press. Doors open at 5:30 PM and the reading and Q & A will take place at 6:30 PM. A book signing will end the evening. Books will be available for purchase at the event.

This event is free and open to the public. RSVP is required. Please email info@gallery1516.org to RSVP.

AUG 8

Open Omaha

Saturday, August 8th — 10am - 4pm

Gallery 1516 is a featured venue in Open Omaha! Open Omaha is a free metro-wide open house event featuring a behind-the-scenes look into the fascinating spaces in our community. Stop by Gallery 1516 on August 8th to learn more about our unique building and our mission for the arts. Discover a history dating back to 1883 and leading up to the world-class art space you know today. And be sure to check out the other spaces across Omaha. See the full list of venues at OpenOmaha.org.
